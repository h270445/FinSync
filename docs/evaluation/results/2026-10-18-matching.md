## 5. Findings

Written with the run above; the numbers are the generated part.

- **Threshold.** 0.85, the current value, is the lowest threshold with no false merge on dev, so it stays. At 0.80 and below two dev false merges appear, both between same-amount Decathlon purchases.
- **The late Decathlon entry.** R0096 is typed with the day of logging, five days after its notification (R0080, outside the window). Two days from it sits another 8990 HUF Decathlon purchase (R0083 + R0088). At 0.80 R0096 is auto-linked into that event; at 0.85 it goes to review against that event instead, so the member is asked and can reject. Its real pair stays missed, the known limit of `very_late_manual`. `tests/test_evaluation_runner.py` guards both outcomes.
- **The other Decathlon case.** R0020 (notification) and R0030 (manual, 31.5 hours later) are two separate 8990 HUF purchases; at 0.80 they merge (score 0.80), at 0.85 R0030 goes to review.
- **Against the baseline (test, arrival order).** Storing every row counts 12 purchases twice. The matcher leaves 4 of them, with no false merge and 11.8% of records in review.
- **Arrival order matters.** In reverse order one false merge appears: the late IKEA entry R0017 arrives before its own notification and is auto-linked to another 14990 HUF IKEA purchase a day away (R0022 + R0024). Real arrival order is close to the forward order, so this is a stress test, but it shows that linking is greedy: a record is never moved when a better match arrives later.
- **Merchant names with a suffix score low.** Bank names such as "IKEA Budaörs" and "MOL Töltőállomás" share half their tokens with the typed "IKEA" and "MOL", so the merchant score is 0.5 instead of 1.0. Because of this, R0069 (MOL, rounded) is sent to review against a different MOL purchase two days away (0.80) instead of its own notification (0.73), and R0024 is reviewed against R0017's event rather than R0022's. Both count as missed duplicates above. A containment measure (shared tokens / the shorter name) would score these 1.0; it is a matcher change, not made in this run.
